FAQ

What people usually ask

How much does it cost?

There's a monthly and a yearly plan, both available in the app. The yearly plan comes with 7 days free: the store doesn't charge you until they're over, and we'll remind you 2 days before.

Does the other person need DebAi to share a list?

Yes. Send them the invite link, they install the app and land right in the list.

Where does my data live?

Your data lives on your phone and, with an active subscription, is backed up to the cloud so you don't lose it when you switch devices.

Does it work with my bank?

On Android, DebAi reads payment notifications from your bank or wallet. If one isn't recognized, you can still log it by voice, photo or by hand.

Can I use several currencies?

Yes. Each expense keeps the currency you paid in, and each list shows the total in the currency you choose.
